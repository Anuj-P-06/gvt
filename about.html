About GVT Engineering

Pioneering Industrial Excellence Since 1978

Founded in 1978, GVT Engineering is an ISO certified company that began its journey as a general fabricator and swiftly earned industry-wide recognition for its superior quality and innovative solutions. Today, we stand as a leading manufacturer and solution provider, with deep expertise in custom-designed industrial equipment, including:

Pressure Vessels (Direct and Indirect Fired)
Hydro-Mechanical Works
Kettles for Hot Dip Galvanizing
Ash Handling / Bulk Material Handling
Storage Tanks
EOT Cranes
Heat Exchangers
ETP/WTP Systems
Heavy & Light Structural Fabrication
